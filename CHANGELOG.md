# Changelog

Every version of Retro Game Library and what changed. Downloads for each one are on the [Releases](../../releases) page.

## 1.4.1 (26 October 2026)
- **Smarter search:** dashes, spaces, dots and accents are ignored, so "zx81" finds Sinclair ZX-81 games and "pokemon" finds Pokémon. System nicknames work too (c64, mega drive, ps2, snes and more), and every word of a multi-word search has to match. Works on the Hardware tab as well.

## 1.4 (26 October 2026)
- **Hardware:** a new Hardware tab for consoles, computers, handhelds, controllers and accessories: model and version, colour or edition, region, serial number, unit only / boxed / complete / sealed, condition, working status, mods and repairs, date, notes and your own photo. Filters by type and by needs-repair. Included in backups, with its own CSV, Excel sheet and PDF section.
- Includes everything from 1.3.

## 1.3 (26 October 2026)
- **Import from a spreadsheet:** Excel (.xlsx) or CSV, with a template that has drop-down lists. Understands most existing spreadsheets: flexible headings, short system names, UK and Excel dates, a Copies column. Shows a summary first, can skip games you already have, and looks up box art and descriptions as it goes.
- **Evercade** added to the systems list.
- (Released on GitHub only; it went to itch.io as part of 1.4.)

## 1.2 (25 October 2026)
- **Look up box art** button in every game: pick the right match from LaunchBox and ZXDB.
- The 100 MB game database is stored once per computer and shared by every copy and version of the app; older copies move theirs automatically.
- A freshly unzipped new version opens straight into your library.

## 1.1 (25 October 2026)
- **Find online…** button for box art: copy a picture from the web and paste it in, or drag it in.
- Rare ZX Spectrum and ZX81 games are also looked up in **ZXDB**, with cover scans and loading screens.

## 1.0 (18 October 2026)
- First release: visual catalogue with box art and descriptions from the LaunchBox Games Database, physical and digital copies, one game on several systems, multiple copies, cloud backup through your synced folder, dated daily backups, and PDF, Excel and CSV export. Windows, Mac, Linux, Raspberry Pi and a browser version.
